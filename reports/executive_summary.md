# Executive Summary

The cleaned analytical data contains 400,916 transactions, 19,213 orders, 4,312 customers, and approximately $8.80M in revenue. Repeat customers represent 67.09% of customers and generate 94.36% of customer revenue. Product revenue is concentrated as well: 887 of 4,015 analyzed products (22.09%) account for approximately 80% of product revenue.

The project combines RFM segmentation, cohort retention, nonparametric statistical testing, predictive retention modeling, future-revenue modeling, product/customer analysis, market-basket analysis, SQL, and executive dashboards. Logistic Regression achieved ROC-AUC 0.7149 for the historical retention task, while the strongest future-revenue model achieved approximately R² 0.528.

The outputs support prioritization and business analysis rather than causal claims. December 2010 is partial through December 9 and is not treated as a complete month in trend comparisons.
